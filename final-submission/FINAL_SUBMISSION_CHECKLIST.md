## Previously blocked items, now resolved

An earlier pass recorded the three binary documents as unregenerable, on the assumption that no document
toolchain existed here. **That assumption was wrong.** `python-pptx`, `python-docx` and `reportlab` are all
available, so:

- `scripts/build_submission.py` rebuilds the `.pptx` and `.docx`
- `scripts/build_pdf.py` rebuilds the `.pdf`
- both read metadata and figures from `scripts/submission_meta.json`, and the bundle size is measured from
  `frontend/dist` rather than hardcoded

All three now carry the current screenshots and the measured counts (910 backend / 72 frontend / 18 browser
workflows). The one caveat that remains is row 19: no human has opened the `.pptx` in PowerPoint.
